About

Bridging real-world networks and intelligent security

I'm a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Old Dominion University, focused on cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, particularly how AI can support more adaptive, autonomous security systems. Before starting my Ph.D., I worked across IT and network support roles, where I gained hands-on experience with network monitoring, troubleshooting, endpoint security, and infrastructure management. I hold an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Networks from the University of New Haven, and I'm drawn to research that stays connected to real operational challenges in security.
